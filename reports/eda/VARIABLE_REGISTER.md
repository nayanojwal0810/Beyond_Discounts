## 1. Population & Subgroup Eligibility Architecture

All analytical calculations must respect the respondent eligibility boundaries established by the questionnaire routing design:

| Subgroup Identifier | Eligibility Criteria / Logical Predicate | Subgroup Size ($n$) | Population Share (%) | Applicable Questions | Key Routing Restrictions |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **All Respondents** | Full Survey Manifest ($N=1,263$) | 1,263 | 100.0% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, 49 | Demographics, awareness, store preference, spend tier, future intent, preferred improvements. |
| **Current Active Users** | `Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency > 1` | 807 | 63.9% | QA 13–45, 46, **47**, 49 | **QA 47 substantive Likert (1–5)**; QA 48 is coded skip flag 11 (`Reason Not Applicable Current User = 1`). |
| **Non-Current Users** | `Ever Ordered == 0` OR (`Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency == 1`) | 456 | 36.1% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, **48**, 49 | **QA 48 substantive barrier multi-responses**; **QA 47 coded 6 (Not Applicable)**; historical satisfaction unobserved. |
| ↳ *Never-Users* | `Ever Ordered == 0` AND `Order Frequency == 1 (Never)` | 376 | 29.8% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, 48, 49 | QA 13–23, 25, 27–42, 44–45, 47 hold codebook N/A skip codes. |
| ↳ *Previous Users* | `Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency == 1 (Never)` | 80 | 6.3% | QA 1–26, 41–43, 46, 48, 49 | Retains historical tenure/order basket memory; **QA 36–40 (satisfaction/problems) unobserved (coded N/A)**. |

## 3. Mandatory Semantic & Questionnaire Routing Rules

1. **QA 47 (Continue If Discounts Reduced):**
   - **Eligible Base:** Strictly the **807 current active users**.
   - **Code 6 Semantics:** Code 6 represents `"Not applicable – never used"` for the 456 non-current respondents. **It must never be treated as negative sentiment, unwillingness to continue, or customer churn.**
   - **Analytical Scale:** Substantive analysis uses the 1–5 ordinal Likert scale among current users:
     * $1 =$ Definitely no (severe discount dependency / churn risk)
     * $2 =$ Probably no (moderate discount sensitivity)
     * $3 =$ Not sure (neutral / price-conscious margin)
     * $4 =$ Probably yes (discount-resilient)
     * $5 =$ Definitely yes (highly resilient / value-driven)

2. **QA 46 (Next 3 Month Stated Future Use):**
   - Universal substantive response across all **1,263 respondents**.
   - Measures stated adoption / continuation intention on a 1–5 Likert scale.
   - Must be analyzed across the three distinct subgroups (Never-users $n=376$, Previous users $n=80$, Current users $n=807$) to evaluate customer acquisition vs retention pipelines.
   - Must **not** be equated with transactional repeat purchase or realized customer retention.

3. **QA 48 (Reasons for Non-Use):**
   - Substantive multi-response binary items applicable to all **456 non-current users**.
   - Analysis must distinguish between **Never-Users ($n=376$)** (acquisition friction) and **Previous Users ($n=80$)** (churn/lapse triggers).
   - Current users ($n=807$) hold the designated skip flag: `Reason Not Applicable Current User == 1`.

4. **QA 49 (Preferred Service Improvement):**
   - Universal single-choice substantive response across all **1,263 respondents** (Options 1–10).
   - Identifies desired platform enhancements (e.g., lower prices, wider range, faster delivery, product quality, refunds).
   - Serves as the primary bridge between consumer pain points and product/CRM roadmap prioritization.

5. **Substantive Responses in General Sections:**
   - **QA 24 (Physical Store Preferences):** Substantive multi-response across all 1,263 respondents. There is no skip code in the questionnaire.
   - **QA 26 (Monthly Quick-Commerce Spend):** Code 1 designates substantive ₹0 spend (non-users and lapsed users), not missing data.
   - **QA 43 (Role in Shopping Routine):** Code 6 designates `"I do not use quick commerce"`, representing a substantive non-use response.
