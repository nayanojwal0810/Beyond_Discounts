# Variable & Question Register: Indian Quick-Commerce Survey

**Canonical Project Title:** *Beyond Discounts: Customer Segmentation and Retention Strategy for Indian Quick Commerce*
**Controlling Dataset:** `data/raw/survey/Data.xlsx` (Worksheet `Data`, 1,263 rows × 130 columns)
**Primary Instrument:** `data/raw/survey/Code Book.docx` & `data/raw/survey/Questionairre.docx` (Chandra, Mendeley Data, DOI: `10.17632/cw88nb7822.1`)
**Document Purpose:** Authoritative, codebook-verified data dictionary detailing all 130 survey variables, valid response codes, eligible populations, source-defined N/A handling, multi-response decomposition, and analytical governance for Gate 1A and subsequent phases.

## 1. Population & Subgroup Eligibility Architecture

All analytical calculations must respect the respondent eligibility boundaries established by the questionnaire routing design:

| Subgroup Identifier | Eligibility Criteria / Logical Predicate | Subgroup Size ($n$) | Population Share (%) | Applicable Questions | Key Routing Restrictions |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **All Respondents** | Full Survey Manifest ($N=1,263$) | 1,263 | 100.0% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, 49 | Demographics, platform awareness/trial, offline preferences, spend tier, future intent, preferred improvements. |
| **Current Active Users** | `Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency > 1` | 807 | 63.89% | QA 13–45, 46, **47**, 49 | **QA 47 substantive Likert (1–5)**; QA 48 holds binary indicator `Reason Not Applicable Current User == 1` (QA 48 option 11). |
| **Non-Current Users** | `Ever Ordered == 0` OR (`Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency == 1`) | 456 | 36.11% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, **48**, 49 | **QA 48 substantive barrier multi-responses**; **QA 47 coded 6 (Not Applicable)**; historical satisfaction unobserved. |
| ↳ *Never-Users* | `Ever Ordered == 0` AND `Order Frequency == 1 (Never)` | 376 | 29.77% | QA 1–12, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, 48, 49 | QA 13–23, 25, 27–42, 44–45, 47 hold codebook N/A skip codes. |
| ↳ *Previous Users* | `Ever Ordered == 1` AND `Order Frequency == 1 (Never)` | 80 | 6.33% | QA 1–14, 15, 24, 26, 43, 46, 48, 49 | Retains historical tenure/trial memory; **QA 36–40 (satisfaction/problems) unobserved (coded N/A)**. |

## 3. Mandatory Questionnaire Routing & Semantics Ground Rules

1. **Multi-Response Questions vs Stored Indicator Dummies:**
   - Multi-select survey items (QA 11, QA 12, QA 18, QA 22, QA 24, QA 31, QA 36, QA 48) are recorded in the dataset as distinct individual binary indicator columns (`0` or `1`).
   - For each indicator column:
     * `1` indicates that the specific option was selected/endorsed by the respondent.
     * `0` indicates that the specific option was not selected.
   - The original questionnaire option codes (e.g. 1–8 in QA 11) identify the response item in the questionnaire instrument, while the stored column holds a binary {0, 1} value.

2. **QA 47 (Continue If Discounts Reduced):**
   - **Eligible Population:** Strictly the **807 current active users**.
   - **Code 6 Semantics:** Code 6 represents `"Not applicable – never used"` for the 456 non-current respondents. **It is an explicit questionnaire routing skip code; it must never be interpreted as negative sentiment, unwillingness to continue, or customer defection.**
   - **Substantive Scale:** Codes 1–5 measure stated continuation likelihood among active shoppers:
     * $1 =$ Definitely no
     * $2 =$ Probably no
     * $3 =$ Not sure
     * $4 =$ Probably yes
     * $5 =$ Definitely yes
   - **Neutral Operational Groupings:** Analytical summaries must use neutral operational labels (e.g., lower stated continuation intention [Codes 1–2], uncertain [Code 3], higher stated continuation intention [Codes 4–5]) rather than unverified business claims (e.g. "severe churn risk").

3. **QA 46 (Next 3 Month Stated Future Use):**
   - Universal substantive response across all **1,263 respondents** (1 = Very unlikely to 5 = Very likely).
   - Measures stated adoption/continuation intention across Never-Users ($n=376$), Previous Users ($n=80$), and Current Active Users ($n=807$).
   - Must be reported as self-reported intention, not realized retention or proven transactional purchase.

4. **QA 48 (Reasons for Non-Use):**
   - Substantive multi-response binary items applicable to all **456 non-current users** (376 never-users + 80 previous users).
   - For Current Active Users ($n=807$), the designated skip flag column `Reason Not Applicable Current User` is coded `1`, while barrier columns are `0`.
   - Statistical comparisons between never-users and previous users must be reported as descriptive/exploratory, acknowledging the limited sample size of the previous-user cohort ($n=80$). Non-significant tests do not establish equivalence, nor do cross-sectional barrier endorsements establish historical causes of discontinuation.

5. **QA 49 (Preferred Service Improvement):**
   - Universal single-choice substantive response across all **1,263 respondents** (Options 1–10).
   - Identifies the primary enhancement most likely to increase the respondent's quick-commerce usage.

6. **Substantive Non-Zero and Non-Use Responses:**
   - **QA 24 (Physical Store Preferences):** Substantive multi-response across all 1,263 respondents. There is no skip code in the questionnaire; 0 means unselected.
   - **QA 26 (Monthly Spend):** Code 1 designates substantive ₹0 spend (non-users and lapsed users), not missing data.
   - **QA 43 (Shopping Routine Role):** Code 6 designates `"I do not use quick commerce"`, representing a substantive non-use response.
